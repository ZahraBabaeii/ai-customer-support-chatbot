# Returns, Refunds, and Exchanges Policy

## Return Window and Eligibility

You can return most products within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund. To be eligible, an item must be:

- in its original packaging, with all accessories, cables, and manuals included;
- in new or like-new condition, without damage beyond what is needed to try it out;
- accompanied by your order number.

Holiday purchases made between November 1 and December 24 can be returned until January 31 of the following year.

## Items That Cannot Be Returned

- **Opened earbuds and ear tips:** for hygiene reasons, Nimbus Pulse Buds and replacement ear tips can only be returned if the box is unopened, unless the product is defective.
- **Gift cards:** digital gift cards are final sale.
- **Gift wrapping fees:** the $4.00 gift wrapping fee is not refundable.
- **Items marked "Final Sale":** clearance products marked as final sale cannot be returned or exchanged.

Defective products are always covered by our warranty, even if they fall into one of these categories.

## How to Start a Return

1. Go to nimbusgear.example/returns and enter your order number and email address.
2. Select the items you want to return and the reason.
3. Print the prepaid return label, or show the QR code at a UPS location.
4. Pack the items in their original packaging and drop the package off within 7 days.

If you cannot use the online returns page, email support@nimbusgear.example and we will set up the return for you.

## Return Shipping Costs

- **US customers:** return shipping is free. We provide a prepaid UPS return label.
- **International customers:** return shipping is paid by the customer. We recommend a tracked service, because we cannot refund packages that are lost on their way back to us.
- **Defective or incorrect items:** we cover return shipping for all customers, including international.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Once your return arrives at our warehouse, we inspect it within 2 business days and then issue the refund. It can take a further 5 to 7 business days for the refund to appear on your statement, depending on your bank or card issuer.

- Original shipping charges are not refunded, unless the item was defective or we sent the wrong product.
- Orders paid with a gift card are refunded as store credit.
- If an item is returned used, damaged, or missing parts, we may deduct a restocking fee of up to 20% of the item price.

## Exchanges

We offer free exchanges for a different color or switch type of the same product within 30 days of delivery. Start an exchange at nimbusgear.example/returns and select "Exchange". We ship the replacement as soon as the carrier scans your return package.

To exchange for a different product, please return the original item for a refund and place a new order.

## Returning Gifts

If you received a Nimbus Gear product as a gift, you can return it for store credit using the order number on the gift receipt. The original purchaser is not notified.

## Returning Bundles

Bundles must be returned as a complete set to receive a full refund. If you return only part of a bundle, the bundle discount is deducted from your refund.
