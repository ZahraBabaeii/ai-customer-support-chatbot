# Warranty and Repairs

## Warranty Coverage

Every Nimbus Gear product comes with a 2-year limited warranty from the date of delivery. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use, including:

- batteries that hold less than 80% of their original capacity within the warranty period;
- buttons, switches, ports, or zippers that stop working;
- audio, charging, or connectivity faults that are not caused by damage.

## What the Warranty Does Not Cover

- Accidental damage, such as drops, spills, or crushing.
- Water damage beyond the product's stated IP rating.
- Normal wear and tear, such as scuffs, scratches, and worn ear cushions.
- Products bought from unauthorized sellers or second-hand.
- Damage caused by third-party repairs or modifications.

## How to Make a Warranty Claim

1. Email support@nimbusgear.example with your order number, a description of the problem, and a photo or short video if possible.
2. Our team replies within 1 business day, usually with a few troubleshooting steps.
3. If the product is defective, we send a prepaid shipping label and ship a replacement, or repair the product, at no cost to you.

Replacements are covered for the remainder of the original warranty period, or 90 days, whichever is longer.
