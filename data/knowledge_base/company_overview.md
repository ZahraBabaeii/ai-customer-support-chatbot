# About Nimbus Gear

## Who We Are

Nimbus Gear is an online store that designs and sells tech accessories for work and travel: headphones, keyboards, chargers, bags, and desk gear. We were founded in 2017 in Portland, Oregon, by two former product designers who wanted everyday tech accessories that were well made, fairly priced, and easy to repair.

Nimbus Gear sells exclusively online through nimbusgear.example. We do not operate physical retail stores, and our products are not sold through third-party marketplaces.

## Our Mission and Values

Our mission is to make dependable gear that lasts for years rather than months.

- **Built to last:** Every Nimbus Gear product is covered by a 2-year limited warranty.
- **Fair pricing:** We sell directly to customers, with no retail markup.
- **Lower impact:** All packaging is plastic-free and recyclable, and our bags are made from 100% recycled nylon.
- **Real support:** Our support team is based in Portland and answers every message personally.

## Services We Offer

- **Free standard shipping** on US orders of $50 or more.
- **30-day returns** on most products, with a free prepaid return label for US customers.
- **2-year limited warranty** on all Nimbus Gear products.
- **Gift wrapping** for $4.00 per item, including a handwritten note.
- **Digital gift cards** from $25 to $250, delivered by email. Gift cards never expire.
- **Business and bulk orders:** orders of 10 or more units of the same product receive a 12% discount. Email business@nimbusgear.example for a quote.
- **Trade-in recycling:** send us any old headphones or chargers (any brand) for free recycling and receive a $10 store credit.
