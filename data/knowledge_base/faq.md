# Frequently Asked Questions

## Payment Methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Nimbus Gear gift cards. You can also pay in 4 interest-free installments with Klarna on orders between $50 and $1,000. We do not accept cash on delivery, checks, or cryptocurrency.

Your card is charged when you place your order.

## Sales Tax

Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on your shipping address, as required by US state law. Prices shown on the website do not include sales tax. International orders are not charged US sales tax, but may be subject to import duties and VAT in the destination country.

## Do I Need an Account to Order?

No. You can check out as a guest using only your email address. Creating a free account lets you track orders, save addresses, and start returns more quickly.

## Resetting Your Password

Go to nimbusgear.example/account, select "Forgot password", and enter your email address. You will receive a reset link that is valid for 60 minutes. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder or contact support.

## Using Discount Codes and Gift Cards

Enter your discount code or gift card number in the "Promo code or gift card" field at checkout. Only one discount code can be used per order, but a gift card can be combined with a discount code. Discount codes cannot be applied after an order has been placed.

## Stock and Back-Orders

If a product is out of stock, you can enter your email address on the product page to be notified when it is available again. Restocks usually take 2 to 4 weeks. We do not take back-orders or pre-orders for out-of-stock products.

## Price Adjustments

If the price of an item you bought drops within 14 days of your order date, contact support with your order number and we will refund the difference. Price adjustments do not apply to Black Friday or Cyber Monday promotions, or to clearance items.

## Product Compatibility

- **Headphones and earbuds** work with any Bluetooth device, including iPhone, Android, Windows, and macOS.
- **The Type 75 keyboard and Glide mouse** work with Windows, macOS, Linux, iPadOS, and Android.
- **The Volt 65 charger** supports USB Power Delivery and charges phones, tablets, and most USB-C laptops that need 65 W or less.
- **The Hub 8-in-1** requires a USB-C port that supports video output (DisplayPort Alt Mode) to use the HDMI port.

## Companion App and Firmware Updates

The free Nimbus Connect app for iOS and Android lets you update firmware, adjust the equalizer, and customize controls on Aero Pro headphones and Pulse Buds. The Type 75 keyboard and Glide mouse are customized using the Nimbus Connect web app at nimbusgear.example/connect. The app is optional; all products work without it.

## Do You Have Physical Stores?

No. Nimbus Gear is an online-only store. We do not have retail locations or a showroom, and our warehouse in Portland is not open to the public for pickup.

## Data Privacy

We only use your personal information to process orders and provide support. We never sell customer data to third parties. Payment details are handled by our payment processor and are never stored on our servers. You can request a copy or deletion of your data by emailing privacy@nimbusgear.example.

## Talking to a Human

Our chat assistant can answer questions about products, shipping, returns, and store policies, but it cannot look up orders, process payments, or change account details. To reach a person, email support@nimbusgear.example or call 1-800-555-0142 during support hours.
