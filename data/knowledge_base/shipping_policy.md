# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Order Processing

Orders are processed within 1 business day. Orders placed before 1:00 PM Pacific Time on a business day usually ship the same day. Orders placed on weekends or US public holidays are processed on the next business day.

You will receive a confirmation email when you place your order and a second email with a tracking number once it ships.

## US Shipping Options and Costs

We ship to all 50 US states, Washington D.C., and APO/FPO addresses.

- **Standard shipping:** 3 to 5 business days. Free on orders of $50 or more; $5.95 on orders under $50.
- **Expedited shipping:** 2 business days. $12.95.
- **Overnight shipping:** next business day when ordered before 1:00 PM Pacific Time. $24.95.

Expedited and overnight shipping are not available for PO boxes or APO/FPO addresses. Shipping to Alaska and Hawaii may take 2 to 3 extra business days.

## International Shipping

We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union, Australia, and New Zealand.

- **Cost:** flat rate of $19.95 per order. International shipping is free on orders of $150 or more.
- **Delivery time:** 7 to 14 business days, depending on the destination and customs processing.
- **Duties and taxes:** import duties, VAT, and customs fees are not included in our prices and are the responsibility of the customer. They are usually collected by the carrier on delivery.

We do not currently ship to countries outside this list.

## Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive an email with a tracking number and a link to the carrier's tracking page. Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to update after the email is sent. You can also track an order at nimbusgear.example/track using your order number and email address.

Our chat assistant cannot look up individual orders. For help with a specific order, contact our support team with your order number.

## Carriers

US orders ship with UPS or USPS. International orders ship with DHL Express. The carrier is selected automatically based on the destination and package size.

## Lost, Delayed, or Damaged Packages

- **Delayed:** if your tracking has not updated for 5 business days, contact us and we will open an investigation with the carrier.
- **Lost:** if a package is confirmed lost, we will send a free replacement or issue a full refund, whichever you prefer.
- **Marked delivered but not received:** check with neighbors and your building's mail room, then contact us within 7 days of the delivery date.
- **Damaged in transit:** contact us within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the damage. We will send a replacement at no cost.

## Changing or Cancelling an Order

You can change or cancel an order within 1 hour of placing it by contacting support. After 1 hour the order may already be packed, and it can no longer be changed. In that case you can return it once it arrives under our 30-day return policy.

To change a shipping address after an order has shipped, you will need to contact the carrier directly using your tracking number.
